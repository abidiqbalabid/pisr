Academics

Academic Curriculum & Syllabus

Directly integrated with the Federal Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (FBISE), Islamabad.

Academic Structure & Scheme of Studies

Pakistan International School Riyadh provides a comprehensive educational framework structured into four main academic wings. The curriculum is regularly updated to satisfy both FBISE educational standards and Saudi Ministry of Education regulatory requirements.

Pre-School & Junior Wing (Playgroup - Grade 5)

Focuses on foundational language proficiency, audio-visual cognitive skills, basic numeracy, and moral development.

  • Playgroup / Nursery / KG: Motor development, language arts, basic numbers, and activity-based learning.
  • Grade 1 to 5: English, Urdu, Mathematics, General Science, Islamic Studies, Social Studies, and Arabic Language.

Middle Wing (Grade 6 - Grade 8)

Strengthens analytical capabilities in preparation for secondary board examinations.

  • Core Subjects: Mathematics, Physics/Chemistry/Biology foundations, English Literature & Grammar, Urdu.
  • Compulsory Studies: Computer Studies, Islamic Studies, Pakistan Studies, and KSA History/Geography.

Secondary Wing (SSC - Grade 9 & 10)

Formal preparation for FBISE Secondary School Certificate (SSC) Examinations.

  • Science Group: Physics, Chemistry, Biology / Computer Science, Mathematics.
  • Humanities Group: General Science, General Mathematics, Computer Studies / Electives.

Higher Secondary Wing (HSSC - Grade 11 & 12)

Specialized academic streams leading to university entrance qualifications.

  • Pre-Medical: Physics, Chemistry, Biology.
  • Pre-Engineering: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics.
  • ICS / Commerce: Computer Science, Economics, Accounting, Business Mathematics, Principles of Commerce.

Assessment & Examination Program

Continuous evaluation, term examinations, and annual FBISE board assessments.

Monthly & Term Evaluation

Continuous assessment includes regular class tests, monthly evaluation assignments, and term examinations conducted at the end of First Term and Final Term.

FBISE Board Examinations

For Grades 9 through 12, examinations are conducted strictly according to the Federal Board (FBISE) Islamabad schedule, with PISR serving as an official external board center.

Promotion & Grading Standards

Students must maintain a minimum required attendance and aggregate pass percentage across all core subjects to qualify for promotion to the subsequent academic grade.

Admissions

Admission FAQs & Regulations

Official guidelines and entry requirements under PISR Rulebook Chapter 5.

Who is eligible for admission to PISR? Rule 5.1.1

Admission is restricted to Pakistani Nationals holding a valid Resident Identity (Iqama) with a minimum age requirement of 2 years and 9 months for the lowest entry level (Play Group/Nursery).

When and where are entry tests conducted? Rule 5.2.4

Admissions are granted based on registration priority and candidate performance in the Admission Test. Tests are conducted every Sunday and Wednesday at 8:00 AM at the school campus.

What documents are required for registration? Rule 5.2.5

Required documents include:

  • Valid Resident Identity (Iqama) copies for student and guardian.
  • Passport copy of the student.
  • Previous School Leaving Certificate and Result Card/Reports (for Grade 1 and above).
  • Completed Admission Registration Form & Processing Fee receipt.

What is the student withdrawal policy? Rule 5.5.1

In case of student withdrawal, parents/guardians must submit a written request giving one month’s notice or pay one month's fee in lieu of notice to settle accounts and receive the official School Leaving Certificate.

Governance

PISR Rules, Regulations & Policies

Official institutional guidelines governing administration, discipline, and procurement.

Discipline Policy: Rule 9.5.10

Get-Together Parties & Treats: Student get-together parties or treats are strictly non-permissible inside the school campus.

Gifts & Commercial Transactions: The exchange of personal gifts, as well as buying or selling items among students, is strictly prohibited.

Enforcement: Violations incur monetary fines and written warnings proportional to the nature of the infraction.

Procurement Regulations (Chapter 4)

Purchases SAR 2,000 to SAR 50,000 (Rule 4.9)

Requires a minimum of three competitive quotations from local suppliers, verified by the Purchasing Committee and Principal prior to SMC approval.

Purchases Above SAR 50,000 (Rule 4.10)

Mandatory public tender advertisement in local newspapers prior to committee review and contract award.
